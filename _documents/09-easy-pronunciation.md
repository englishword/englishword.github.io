This section revisits the pronunciation of words in the order of the 26 letters, showing how words are "sounded out" from letters.

## A a

- Open sound [ā]: e.g. nāme.
- Open combinations [āi], [āy]: e.g. rāin, wāy.
- Other combinations [ãu], [ãw]: e.g. sãuse, lãw.
- Closed sound [a]: e.g. bad.
- [à] sound: clàss, glàss.
- [àr] sound: e.g. càr.
- Short weak [å]: e.g. åbòut.
- Light [ȧ]: e.g. imȧge.

## B b

- Same as Chinese pinyin [b].

## C c

- Before a, o, u and at word end, pronounced [k].
- Before e, i, y, pronounced [s].
- ch, tch combinations pronounced [ch], e.g. chip, catch.
- ch rarely pronounced [sh], e.g. machine, chicago.

## D d

- Same as Chinese pinyin [d].
- dg combination pronounced [j], e.g. judge.

## E e

- Open sound [ē]: e.g. mē.
- Vowel combinations [ēe], [ēa], [iē], [ēi], [ēy].
- Closed sound [e]: e.g. bed.
- Long weak [e̊r]: e.g. berg.
- Short weak [e̊]: [ǝ] sound.
- Light [ė]: e.g. begin.
- Silent: word-final e is usually silent.

## F f

- Same as Chinese pinyin [f].

## G g

- Before a, o, u and at word end, pronounced [g].
- Before e, i, y, pronounced [j]; a few high-frequency words are exceptions, still [g]: get, girl, tiger, begin, finger, forget, give.
- gh combination:
  - Usually silent, e.g. light.
  - Rare exceptions pronounced [f], e.g. laugh, tough.
- In ng combination, pronounced /ŋ/, e.g. king.

## H h

- Same as Chinese pinyin [h].
- In some words, h is silent, e.g. what, hour.

## I i

- Open sound [ī]: e.g. nice.
- Closed sound [i]: e.g. sit.
- Long weak [i̊r]: e.g. bird.
- Short weak [i̊]: e.g. evil
- Light [i]: e.g. lily.
- Long sound [ĩ]: e.g. police.

## J j

- Same as Chinese pinyin [j].

## K k

- Same as Chinese pinyin [k].
- In words starting with kn, k is silent, e.g. knife.

## L l

- Same as Chinese pinyin [l].

## M m

- Same as Chinese pinyin [m].

## N n

- Same as Chinese pinyin [n].

## O o

- Open sound: e.g. go.
- Open combinations: [oa], [ow].
- Closed sound: e.g. hot.
- Long weak: replaced, pronounced /or/, e.g. force.
- Short weak: /ǝ/ sound.
- Vowel combinations: [oo].
- Vowel combinations: [ou], [ow], e.g. loud, cow.
- Vowel combinations: [oi], [oy], e.g. oil, boy.

## P p

- Same as Chinese pinyin [p].
- ph combination pronounced [f], e.g. photo.

## Q q

- qu combination often at word start, pronounced /kw/.

## R r

- Same as Chinese pinyin r.
- Open + R: a, e, i, o, u combine as ar, er, ir, or, ur.
- Closed + R:
- Weak + R:

## S s

- Same as Chinese pinyin s.
- Pronounced [z].
- sh combination pronounced [sh].
- As a suffix, e.g. -sion, -sure, pronunciation is fixed; memorize.

## T t

- Same as Chinese pinyin t.
- th combination:
  - Mostly pronounced [th], e.g. thing.
  - In prepositions, articles, pronouns, conjunctions, pronounced [ṫh], e.g. the, they.
- As a suffix, e.g. -tion, -ture, pronunciation is fixed; memorize.

## U u

- Open sound: pronounced /yu:/, e.g. duty; after L, R pronounced /u:/, e.g. blue, ruler.
- Combinations: ui, eu, ew.
- Closed sound: e.g. but.
- Long weak: e.g. burn.
- Short weak: /ǝ/ sound.
- Light: rare, e.g. busy, minute.

## V v

- Pronounced [v], e.g. very.

## W w

- As a consonant, same as Chinese pinyin [w].
- As a vowel, equivalent to letter u.
- Combinations: aw, ew, ow.

## X x

- Before a consonant, pronounced [ks].
- Before a vowel, pronounced [gz].
- At word start, pronounced [z].

## Y y

- As a consonant, pronounced [y], e.g. yes.
- As a vowel, equivalent to letter i.

## Z z

- Same as Chinese pinyin z.
