## Symbols Above Single Vowel Letters and Vowel-Letter Combinations

**Three high-frequency primary marks**

- Horizontal line (─): open-sound mark — pronounce the letter sound of the marked vowel letter
- Empty mark (∅): closed-sound mark — pronounce the closed sound corresponding to that vowel letter
- Dot (⦁): light-sound mark — always pronounced as the light sound /i/
- Ring (○): weak-sound mark — always pronounced as the weak sound /ǝ/

**Three low-frequency secondary marks**

- Tilde (∼): long-sound mark
- Backslash (∖): changed-sound mark
- Caret (˄): /ʌ/-sound mark (pronounced /ʌ/)

## Marks Above a Few Consonant Letters

- No mark (∅): the first, most common pronunciation
- Dot (⦁): the second, less common pronunciation
- Tilde (∼): the third, least common pronunciation
