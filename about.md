## About IPSM Approach

IPSM Approach (Integrated Pronunciation, Spelling and Meaning) is an open, method-based approach to English vocabulary learning. It is published openly at [lingose.com](https://lingose.com) in 10 languages and released under the MIT license.

The method is built on the observation that memorizing an English word is, at its core, the process of linking three attributes — pronunciation, spelling, and meaning. IPSM makes that linkage explicit by:

- annotating phonemes so that pronunciation rules become visible in the spelling (IPS),
- segmenting morphemes so that word-formation rules and core roots are exposed (ISM),
- and connecting the rhythm of phonemes to meaning (IPM).

### Coverage

The lexicon covers 6,099 English words grouped into 2,806 root-lemma families. The full method is presented across 9 chapters, from IPS symbols and notation through the three unifying approaches.
